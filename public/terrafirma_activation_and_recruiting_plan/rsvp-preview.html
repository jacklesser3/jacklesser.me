Keep Austin Building.

TerraFirma Playground is planned for East Austin in spring 2027. We’ll confirm the date and venue before registration opens.

Operate the machine. Meet the crew.

Come see how TerraFirma moves dirt. An operator and an engineer will show how they use Mission Control to run a machine from a screen. Ask about a coached turn, meet the hiring team, or bring a question about your next excavation or grading project.

Admission is free. RSVP once and show your ticket when you arrive between 5 and 9 p.m. Central. Once inside, you can join the demo queue by QR code or ask a host to add you. Coached turns are limited and subject to TerraFirma approval. You can also watch from the rail or on the big screen, try the simulator, or join the fireside.

What to expect

5:00 p.m. Doors and demo queue open
6:10 p.m. Narrated machine demo
6:30–6:50 p.m. Fireside and audience Q&A
9:00 p.m. Event closes

We’re planning Texas BBQ hors d’oeuvres from Johnny Ochoa, plus water and nonalcoholic drinks. Elevate will serve beer if the venue approves. If you’re taking a coached turn, save the beer for afterward. Wear comfortable shoes for the outdoor demo and tell us about any access needs when you register.

Registration setup

Publish one free general-admission ticket from TerraFirma’s approved calendar once the date, location, demo format, and venue capacity are confirmed. Set the event for 5–9 p.m. Central (America/Chicago). Release tickets in batches based on the venue’s capacity and keep the demo queue separate from registration.

Ask guests whether they want to meet the team, learn about jobs, or discuss a project. Leave role, company, and access needs optional and ask separately for permission to follow up. Collect phone numbers only from people who want demo waitlist texts. Put the guest guide in the confirmation. Include parking, access, weather, and demo updates in the reminders.
