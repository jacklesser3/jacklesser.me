Why join TerraFirma?

At TerraFirma, the people running the equipment work with the people building the software. Together with project teams, they improve how crews clear land, excavate, and level sites.

Field and equipment

What field crews do

Operators bring experience digging, leveling ground, and handling equipment to jobs that use Mission Control, TerraFirma’s system for coordinating and running machines remotely. Field engineers help crews read digital plans, check the ground against the target height, and fix problems with the tools they use on site.

Ask the crew: What does a good grading pass look like, and what can an operator spot that a screen might miss?

Engineering and controls

What engineers build and test

Engineers build software and controls, connect sensors to heavy equipment, and test how it all works together. Operators help them decide what needs to be visible on screen and what to improve before the next job.

Ask the team: What should a remote operator see first, and how would you test that it helps on a real job?

Projects and delivery

How project teams plan the work

Estimators, civil engineers, and field crews use drawings and site conditions to figure out how much dirt to move, where it needs to go, and in what order to do the work. TerraFirma’s published projects include excavation in North Austin and preparing ground for buildings in Taylor.

Ask a project lead: What slows the first day of sitework, and what do you want to know before you price a job?
