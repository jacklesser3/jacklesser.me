Proposed experience: book a timed slot, meet the team, see one approved machine task, and explore relevant Texas roles. Public participation depends on engineering and EHS approval; an operator-led demonstration or clearly labeled simulator is the fallback.

Ownership: the producer handles venue, accessibility, permits, insurance, and staffing; engineering and EHS approve control behavior, supervision, separation, stop procedures, and weather limits. A qualified operator retains control authority. Recruiting owns consenting candidate follow-up.

04 / The recommendation

A direction to believe in.
A pilot to learn from.

This is a proposal. The next step is to align on the creative direction and turn the Austin scope into a quoted, executable plan.

01

Back the creative direction.

Texas-built ambition, a Mars horizon, and a distinctive local crew. Confirm the shared neon system and the city-specific character.

02

Develop the Austin pilot.

Use the proposed budget envelope to obtain inventory and vendor quotes. Confirm priority roles, the recruiting destination, and a feasible demonstration.

Explore measurement and delivery responsibilities+

Before launch, recruiting defines priority Texas roles, qualification criteria, a maximum cost per qualified screen, a minimum evaluable cohort, and a follow-up owner. Compare the campaign with the prior 90-day funnel and a relevant comparison channel or market where practical.

Track message recall, relevant role visits, qualified applications, completed screens, interviews, offers, acceptances, and starts. Record first-touch, assisted, and self-reported source; billboard exposure cannot be individually attributed. Measure retention 90 days after each hire’s start date.

Expand only when candidate quality and acquisition cost meet the agreed criteria and delivery works reliably. Weak or incomplete evidence calls for improving the test, not automatically adding cities.

Brand + recruiting: message, role brief, destination, and candidate follow-up. Producer + engineering + EHS: venue, permits, equipment, operating plan, and rehearsal. Finance + leadership: all-in quotes, staged commitments, and the expansion decision.
