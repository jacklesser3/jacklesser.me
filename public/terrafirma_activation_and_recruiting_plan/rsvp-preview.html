Keep Austin Building.

TerraFirma Playground · East Austin · Spring 2027 · Date and venue pending

Drive the machine. Meet the crew.

Come see how TerraFirma moves dirt. Watch a field operator and controls engineer show how they work together, ask about taking a coached turn with Mission Control, then meet the people behind the equipment. Talk shop over tacos, meet the hiring team or bring a question about your next earthworks project.

Admission is free. RSVP for a suggested arrival window. Machine turns are limited and depend on the operating mode approved for the day. You can watch from the rail, try the simulator, or join the fireside at 5:55 p.m. Central.

What to expect

3:30 p.m. Doors and first arrival window
4:45 p.m. Operator and engineer walkthrough
5:55–6:15 p.m. Fireside and audience Q&A
8:30 p.m. Event closes

Wear comfortable shoes suitable for an outdoor venue. Seating, water and an accessible guest route are planned. Share access needs when registering so a host can follow up privately.

Registration setup

Publish from the approved TerraFirma calendar when the date, venue address, operating mode and room limits are confirmed. Set America/Chicago and four free arrival windows at 3:30, 4:50, 5:30 and 6:30 p.m., with capacities set against the approved concurrent occupancy model. Use waitlist or manual approval for overflow. A timed arrival is separate from a machine turn.

Ask for primary interest (meet the team, careers, projects), optional role or company, optional access needs, and separate permission for future outreach. Collect mobile numbers only through the optional demo queue. Link the guest guide in the confirmation and include parking, access, weather and demo-mode updates in reminders.
