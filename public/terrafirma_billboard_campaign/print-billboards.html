TerraFirma / Production notes18 November 2026 · Revision 3

Readable at a glance.

  • Three-second comprehension check with people outside the project: headline, TerraFirma, and Texas hiring invitation.
  • Test a reduced proof and the actual media-owner sightline at its expected viewing distance, speed, and lighting.
  • Require the headline, wordmark, and URL to remain legible with glow switched off. Keep glow outside the letters; preserve counters and spacing.
  • Have the printer proof orange, dark edges, fine illustration detail, safe area, and material under day and night conditions.
  • These are creative masters, not signed-off mechanical files. Recompose for each vendor’s dimensions and safe zones; do not stretch the board.
  • Keep one headline, one brand, one action, and one short URL. No QR code on high-speed road inventory.
  • Miami needs fluent Spanish review and a matching bilingual recruiting path before launch.
  • Illustration establishes the world. Approved photography and product demonstrations supply the evidence on the destination and in follow-up content.

Official TerraFirma identity. Illustrated Operator character. All boards use the existing company URL; the recruiting destination must be ready before launch.
