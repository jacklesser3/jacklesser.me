The chrome Operator, warm bone ground, Mars orange, luminous headline, excavator, skyline, and rocket form the common visual system. Pose, workwear, helmet detail, city silhouettes, and small rim-light accents create local character.

The headline uses a saturated orange letter body, a deeper orange edge, and a narrow amber highlight. A tighter orange glow keeps the neon feeling while the solid color carries the reading weight. Keep the wordmark and hiring line clean. Check the actual media-owner sightline and a three-second read, then proof day and night color, safe areas, and vendor dimensions before production.

These are proposal concepts. The Operator is an illustrated brand character, not a claimed TerraFirma humanoid product. See the simple test sequence+
  1. Prepare: confirm priority Texas roles, choose two relevant Austin faces, obtain written all-in quotes, and establish the current recruiting baseline.
  2. Before launch: finalize the artwork to vendor dimensions, verify the careers journey and source tracking, and approve the production proof. Set the start date around inventory and posting lead times.
  3. Run for four weeks: use one Austin creative on both faces; check posting, readability, site traffic, and candidate-source data. This tests the campaign as a whole, not an A/B comparison.
  4. Review: assess response when the flight ends; follow the candidate funnel at 30, 60, and 90 days after it ends. Use the results to shape any next investment.

Dates remain to be quoted and confirmed. The test is not tied to SXSW or another event. Live experiences and additional markets remain future options.

Explore measurement and delivery responsibilities+

Before launch, recruiting defines priority Texas roles, qualification criteria, a maximum cost per qualified screen, a minimum evaluable cohort, and a follow-up owner. Compare the campaign with the prior 90-day funnel and a relevant comparison channel or market where practical.

Track message recall, relevant role visits, qualified applications, completed screens, interviews, offers, acceptances, and starts. Record first-touch, assisted, and self-reported source; billboard exposure cannot be individually attributed. Measure retention 90 days after each hire’s start date.

Expand only when candidate quality and acquisition cost meet the agreed criteria and delivery works reliably. Weak or incomplete evidence calls for improving the test, not automatically adding cities.

Brand + recruiting: creative, roles, the careers journey, and follow-up. Media owner + production: inventory, print proofs, posting, and removal. Finance + leadership: all-in quotes and the decision on a next test.
