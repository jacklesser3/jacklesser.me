Keep Austin Building.

Proposed for April 7, 2027, from 5 to 8:30 p.m. Central, with April 14 as backup. Distribution Hall in East Austin is the recommended location, pending written approval for attendees to operate the excavator with a TerraFirma coach. If approval falls through, the pilot moves to the Robot Ranch in Buda, subject to availability. Registration opens after the site and date are confirmed.

Watch an operator and an engineer explain how a crew shapes the ground to match a site plan. See the equipment up close, ask a recruiter about jobs, or bring the project team a question about a site of your own.

Free general admission

One ticket lets you arrive anytime between 5 and 8:30 p.m., explore the exhibit, watch the grading presentation, and meet the crew. You’ll be able to request a coached turn on the real excavator once the venue approves guest operation and the crew confirms how many turns fit. A general admission ticket does not reserve a turn.

Preview the confirmation email

Bring your general admission ticket to check-in. The grading presentation starts at 6:20 p.m. Check your arrival email for venue details, accessibility and weather updates, and how to request a coached turn. If a waiver is required, the team will share it before your turn.

What to expect

5:00 p.m. Doors open.
5:10–6:10 p.m. Proposed window for coached attendee turns.
6:00 p.m. Take a tour of the From Plan to Dirt exhibit.
6:20–6:28 p.m. Watch the crew explain a grading pass.

6:45–6:50 p.m. Ask the speakers a question.
7:45 p.m. Catch the second exhibit tour.
8:30 p.m. The event closes.

Each exhibit tour lasts ten minutes. If the crew can’t show the grading pass live, they can use approved footage from the field. We’re planning to serve Texas barbecue bites and nonalcoholic drinks. Beer service depends on venue approval.

For the registration team

The East Austin model assumes 285 RSVPs for about 200 arrivals. Open registration after the venue approves guest operation in writing and insurance, date and attendance limit are confirmed. Release coached-turn slots after rehearsal and include any waiver requirements in the confirmation. If the pilot moves to Buda, set the cap from the confirmed Robot Ranch capacity before opening registration.
