01 / Brand storyTerraFirma

Built in Texas.
Building toward Mars.

TerraFirma brings software, robotics, and construction teams together to expand what humanity can build. Texas is where we prove it. Mars is where the ambition leads.

The work today

Human-in-the-loop autonomy, remote operations, and better planning on real Texas projects. Show the machinery, the people, and the work accomplished.

The invitation

Build with us in Texas. Invite engineers and builders to work on consequential physical problems, with a direct route to relevant open roles.

The horizon

Bring the capability to build on Earth toward a future on Mars. Present Mars as the long-term mission, never as a claim of current off-world operations.

Campaign platform: Mars starts with earthwork.
Brand signoff: Texas Born, Mars Bound.
Billboard action: Build with us in Texas · terrafirma.inc

Futuristic means precision, technical ambition, and forward motion. Texas means ownership, practical skill, and proof in the field. The Operator is an illustrated brand character; it is not a depiction of a TerraFirma humanoid product.

03 / Visual and copy systemTerraFirma

One world. Clear words.

Keep the chrome Operator, bone canvas, Mars orange, and working machinery. Let the message change by audience; let the brand stay recognizable.

Neon with a solid core

Every headline now uses solid orange letterforms, a dark defining edge, and a restrained outer glow. The words remain intact when the glow disappears. Script and hollow neon lettering are removed.

Headlines are vector paths rather than generated image text. The official TerraFirma wordmark, action line, and web address are also vectors, so they remain crisp at larger sizes.

The standard master is 48:14. Keep every line within the central clear area; do not place lettering over the Operator, excavator, grid, or horizon.

A consistent crew

Austin: the Texas cowboy character, with hat and scarf.
Engineering markets: plain technical workwear.
Field and infrastructure markets: a plain hardhat and work jacket.

Boston loses the scally cap and shamrock. Nashville loses the rhinestone treatment. Other markets lose novelty accessories, invented badges, and visual cues that imply an affiliation.

Local relevance comes from the work and the headline. The same three character treatments carry the entire system.

05 / Field experienceTerraFirma

Let people see
the work happen.

A controlled Mission Control experience connects the future-facing campaign to real engineering and field operations.

Book

Choose a timed slot. State whether the session is a live demonstration, supervised participation, or a simulator.

Brief

Explain the task, the human-in-the-loop system, and the boundaries. A qualified operator retains control authority.

Experience

Watch or complete one approved task in a segregated practice area. No public access to active customer work.

Connect

Meet a builder or engineer, see relevant roles, and choose whether to receive recruiting follow-up.

One machine, honest capacity

Planning model: one machine × 60 minutes ÷ six minutes per slot × 70% operating availability = seven sessions per hour, or 56 over eight hours. The slot includes briefing, activity, and reset. Validate this in rehearsal before releasing appointments.

Staffing breaks, faults, weather, and resets reduce availability. Use timed bookings and a waitlist. Do not promise a zero queue. A second console on the same machine does not double capacity.

Clear demonstration boundaries

Engineering and EHS approve the machine, control path, stop behavior, exclusion area, supervision, and weather limits. Do not substitute a broadcast video link for a validated machine-control connection.

If public operation is not approved, run an operator-led demonstration or clearly labeled simulator. A collectible mission card is a souvenir, not a license, certification, or hiring assessment.

Austin format: one local site, one machine, one console, one day. The pilot budget includes setup, external crew, local logistics, and an operating allowance. A container tour, multi-city playgrounds, and additional pods require separately costed proposals.

Run of show and operating ownership
  • Before opening: producer signs off access, site approvals, accessibility, weather decision, power, and connectivity; engineering and EHS complete the operating rehearsal and stop test.
  • During the eight-hour program: venue host manages timed entry; the qualified operator and safety lead manage each approved session; engineering provides technical support; recruiters hold separate career conversations. Confirm named people and relief coverage in the staffing roster.
  • At close: secure machinery, reconcile incidents and downtime, capture actual costs, and check opt-in records. Use booking contact details for service messages only unless the attendee separately opts into marketing.
  • Within two business days: recruiting follows up with consenting, relevant candidates. Publish only approved footage, with releases where required.

08 / MeasurementTerraFirma

Measure the invitation.
Follow it to a hire.

Before the first impression

Recruiting defines the roles, qualification criteria, target seniority, on-site requirements, and response owner. Establish the prior 90-day funnel and a comparable market or channel where practical.

Agree on a maximum cost per qualified screen using actual recruiting economics. Set a minimum cohort size and a follow-up service level before launch. Do not manufacture a hire forecast from billboard impressions.

One connected journey

Use the short company URL on the board. Before launch, make the campaign and current roles easy to find from that homepage; track the campaign destination, event links, and role handoff.

The current public careers route is TerraFirma’s Ashby board. Any dedicated /careers or campaign redirect must be created and tested by TerraFirma before printing a new address. State Texas work location and only confirmed relocation terms.

Recall

Unaided brand and message recall in a small matched pre/post sample. Report sample size and uncertainty.

Interest

Relevant visits, returning visitors, role views, and demo bookings. Separate media traffic from event and paid digital traffic.

Quality

Qualified applications, completed screens, interview progression, and cost per qualified screen.

Outcome

Offers, acceptances, starts, and 90-day retention by cohort. Track first-touch and assisted source alongside self-report.

Scale rule: Boston proceeds only if the Austin cohort is sufficiently large to evaluate, role quality meets recruiting’s agreed bar, acquisition cost is within the pre-agreed ceiling, and follow-up and event delivery work reliably. If evidence is weak or incomplete, improve or extend the test before expanding.

Billboard exposure is not individually attributable. Use vendor audience estimates as estimates, triangulate with the baseline and comparison, and avoid presenting assisted hires or direct traffic as proven incremental lift.
