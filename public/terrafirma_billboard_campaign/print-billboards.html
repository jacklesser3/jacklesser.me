The shared system

Character, place,
and a clear invitation.

The chrome Operator, warm bone ground, Mars orange, luminous headline, excavator, skyline, and rocket form the common visual system. Pose, workwear, helmet detail, city silhouettes, and small rim-light accents create local character.

The headline uses warm-white lettering, a defined coral-orange edge, and a restrained orange glow. The light core carries the neon feeling without a black letter fill. Keep the wordmark and hiring line clean. Check the actual media-owner sightline and a three-second read, then proof day and night color, safe areas, and vendor dimensions before production.

These are proposal concepts. The Operator is an illustrated brand character, not a claimed TerraFirma humanoid product. Miami needs fluent Spanish review and a matching recruiting journey. Use current Texas roles and confirmed relocation terms. The boards use the existing company URL; any campaign redirect must work before a different address is printed.

The operator, city skyline, rocket, excavator, and neon lettering are proposal illustrations. Verify current roles, final production requirements, and actual viewing conditions before launch.
