Why join TerraFirma?

At TerraFirma, equipment operators help engineers decide what to build. They work with project teams on jobs like clearing land, excavating, and leveling sites. At the event, you can ask each team what that work looks like.

Field and equipment

What field crews do

Operators use their experience digging and leveling ground to run equipment remotely through TerraFirma’s Mission Control system. Field engineers help them read digital plans, check whether the ground is at the right height, and fix problems on site.

Ask an operator, “When you finish a pass, how can you tell whether the ground matches the plan?”

Explore two example roles. Equipment operator · Field engineer

Engineering and controls

What engineers build and test

Engineers connect sensors to heavy equipment and build the software and controls that operators use. They test the system together. An operator can point out what’s hard to see on screen, and an engineer can try a change before the next job.

Ask an engineer, “What did you change after watching someone use the controls?”

Explore engineering at TerraFirma · Read an example integration technician job description

Projects and delivery

How project teams plan the work

Estimators, civil engineers, and field crews study the drawings and the site. Together they work out how much dirt to move, where it needs to go, and which work comes first. TerraFirma’s projects include excavation in North Austin and preparing the ground for buildings in Taylor.

Ask a project lead, “What do you need to know about a site before you can price the work?”
