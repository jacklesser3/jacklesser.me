Keep Austin Building.

TerraFirma Playground is planned for East Austin in spring 2027. We’ll confirm the date and venue before registration opens.

Operate the machine. Meet the crew.

Come see how TerraFirma moves dirt. An operator and an engineer will show how they use Mission Control to run a machine from a screen. Ask about a coached turn, talk with the hiring team over tacos, or bring a question about your next excavation or grading project.

Admission is free. Choose an arrival time when you RSVP and show your confirmation to a host at the entrance. Coached turns are limited and depend on what TerraFirma approves for the day. You can watch from the rail or on the big screen, try the simulator, or come to the fireside at 5:55 p.m. Central.

What to expect

3:30 p.m. Doors and first arrival window
4:45 p.m. Operator and engineer walkthrough
5:55–6:15 p.m. Fireside and audience Q&A
8:30 p.m. Event closes

We’re planning tacos, water, and nonalcoholic drinks, with beer pending venue approval. If you’re taking a coached turn, save the beer for afterward. Wear comfortable shoes for the outdoor demo and tell us about any access needs when you register.

Registration setup

Publish from TerraFirma’s approved calendar once the date, address, demo format, and capacity are confirmed. Use Central time (America/Chicago) and set free arrival windows starting at 3:30, 4:50, 5:30, and 6:30 p.m. Give each window three start times, ten minutes apart, and set the number of places the venue can take. Use a waitlist or approve extra requests by hand. Guests book machine turns separately.

Ask guests whether they want to meet the team, learn about jobs, or discuss a project. Leave role, company, and access needs optional and ask separately for permission to follow up. Collect phone numbers only from people who want demo waitlist texts. Put the guest guide in the confirmation. Include parking, access, weather, and demo updates in the reminders.
