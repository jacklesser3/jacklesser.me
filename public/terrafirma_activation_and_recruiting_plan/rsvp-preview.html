Luma RSVP preview

Keep Austin Building.

We’re planning the event for March 24, 2027, from 5 to 8:30 p.m. Central at Distribution Hall in East Austin. March 31 is the backup date. If Distribution Hall can’t approve guest turns on the full-size excavator, we’ll check whether Robot Ranch in Buda can host us. Registration opens once we’ve confirmed the venue and date and reserved the excavator.

Watch an operator and an engineer explain how they shape the ground to match a site plan. You can also see the equipment up close, ask about jobs or talk with the crew about a project of your own.

Free general admission

RSVP for free admission and drop by the From Plan to Dirt exhibit anytime. If the venue approves guest turns, you can join the line on site starting at 5:00 p.m. The host will give you an estimated wait and stop taking names when all turns are taken.

Preview the confirmation email

Bring your ticket to check-in. Arrive by 6:10 p.m. for the 6:20 p.m. demo. Your arrival email will tell you where to check in and join the excavator line. It will also include accessibility information and weather updates. If you need to sign a waiver, the host will share it before your turn.

What to expect

5:00 p.m. Doors open, along with the exhibit and the line for the excavator.
5:10–6:10 p.m. Guests in line can take a coached turn if the venue approves.
6:10 p.m. Arrive by this time to catch the featured program.
6:20–6:28 p.m. Watch the crew explain how the excavator shapes the ground.
6:30–6:45 p.m. Hear an operator and an engineer discuss a Texas project.
6:45–6:50 p.m. Ask the speakers a question.
8:30 p.m. The event ends.

If the crew can’t run the excavator live, they’ll show approved footage from the field. We plan to serve small bites of Texas barbecue and nonalcoholic drinks. Beer service depends on venue approval.

For the registration team

We expect about 200 guests from 285 RSVPs in East Austin. Before opening registration, confirm the date, reserve the excavator and get written permission for guests to operate it. Confirm the insurance requirements and how many people the venue can hold.

Use Luma for admission and manage excavator turns on site. Have the host give guests an estimated wait, close the line when the remaining turns are taken and share any waiver requirements before each turn. If the event moves to Buda, set the ticket limit based on how many people Robot Ranch can hold.
