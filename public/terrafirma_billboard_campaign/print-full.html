TerraFirma / Field experience18 November 2026 · Revision 3

Let people see
the work happen.

A controlled Mission Control experience connects the future-facing campaign to real engineering and field operations.

Book

Choose a timed slot. State whether the session is a live demonstration, supervised participation, or a simulator.

Brief

Explain the task, the human-in-the-loop system, and the boundaries. A qualified operator retains control authority.

Experience

Watch or complete one approved task in a segregated practice area. No public access to active customer work.

Connect

Meet a builder or engineer, see relevant roles, and choose whether to receive recruiting follow-up.

One machine, honest capacity

Planning model: one machine × 60 minutes ÷ six minutes per slot × 70% operating availability = seven sessions per hour, or 56 over eight hours. The slot includes briefing, activity, and reset. Validate this in rehearsal before releasing appointments.

Staffing breaks, faults, weather, and resets reduce availability. Use timed bookings and a waitlist. Do not promise a zero queue. A second console on the same machine does not double capacity.

Clear demonstration boundaries

Engineering and EHS approve the machine, control path, stop behavior, exclusion area, supervision, and weather limits. Do not substitute a broadcast video link for a validated machine-control connection.

If public operation is not approved, run an operator-led demonstration or clearly labeled simulator. A collectible mission card is a souvenir, not a license, certification, or hiring assessment.

Follow the cohort.

Recall

Unaided brand and message recall in a small matched pre/post sample. Report sample size and uncertainty.

Interest

Relevant visits, returning visitors, role views, and demo bookings. Separate media traffic from event and paid digital traffic.

Quality

Qualified applications, completed screens, interview progression, and cost per qualified screen.

Outcome

Offers, acceptances, starts, and 90-day retention by cohort. Track first-touch and assisted source alongside self-report.

Scale rule: Boston proceeds only if the Austin cohort is sufficiently large to evaluate, role quality meets recruiting’s agreed bar, acquisition cost is within the pre-agreed ceiling, and follow-up and event delivery work reliably.
